Hi! I am a fifth-year Ph.D. student in the Institute of Operations Research and Analytics at National University of Singapore, working with Zhenyu Hu, Joel Goh, and Zhi Chen.

I am broadly interested in how information and incentives shape strategic decision-making under uncertainty. In particular, I study the role of feedback mechanisms and dynamic contracting in innovation sourcing, as well as how policy affects search behavior and outcomes under competition. I also have a growing research interest in healthcare operations management, especially in infectious disease modeling and hospital service system design to improve healthcare delivery. Methodologically, my work draws primarily on game theory and dynamic programming.

I received my B.E. in Industrial Systems Engineering and Management from NUS, with a Minor in Financial Mathematics.

Research Interests

Research Topics: Innovation Sourcing, Feedback Design, Healthcare Operations

Methodologies: Game Theory, Dynamic Programming, Dynamic Contracting
