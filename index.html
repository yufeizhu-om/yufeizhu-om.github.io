Hi! I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in the Institute of Operations Research and Analytics at the National University of Singapore, advised by Zhenyu Hu and Joel Goh.

I study how firms should design dynamic operational processes when participants respond strategically to information, incentives, and one another’s actions. A central challenge is that seemingly appealing policies, such as revealing more information, strengthening incentives, or introducing additional competition, may turn out to be suboptimal once the behavior they induce is taken into account. I address this challenge in contexts including talent recruitment, innovation sourcing, and human–AI systems, drawing on game theory and dynamic programming. More recently, I have been studying healthcare operations in collaboration with practitioners at the National University Health System (NUHS), focusing on care-delivery operations in their large-scale hospital-at-home program.

I received my B.E. in Industrial Systems Engineering and Management from NUS, with a Minor in Financial Mathematics.

Research Interests

Research Topics: Information and Incentive Design, Search and Matching, Human–AI Systems, Healthcare Operations

Methodologies: Game Theory, Dynamic Programming, Dynamic Contracting
