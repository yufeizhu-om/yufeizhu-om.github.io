I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Institute of Operations Research and Analytics at the National University of Singapore, working with Zhenyu Hu, Joel Goh, and Zhi Chen.

My research broadly examines how information and incentives shape strategic decision-making under uncertainty. In particular, I am interested in how feedback mechanisms and contractual arrangements influence innovation sourcing, and how policies affect search outcomes in competitive settings. Methodologically, I draw on applied game theory, dynamic programming, and dynamic contracting. I also have a secondary research interest in healthcare operations, with a focus on infectious disease modelling and improving hospital service systems to enhance healthcare delivery.

I received my B.E. in Industrial Systems Engineering and Management from the National University of Singapore, with a Minor in Financial Mathematics.

Research Interests

Applications: Innovation Sourcing, Healthcare Operations Management, Platform Operations

Methodologies: Applied Game Theory, Dynamic Programming, Dynamic Contracting, Optimization
